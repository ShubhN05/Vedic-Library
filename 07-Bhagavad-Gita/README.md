# Bhagavad Gita

The Bhagavad Gita in **English and Hindi**, as one PDF in each language.

> हिन्दी: श्रीमद्भगवद्गीता, अंग्रेज़ी और हिन्दी दोनों में, एक-एक PDF के रूप में।

## About this book

- **Where the Gita comes from:** the Bhagavad Gita is not a separate book. It is a part of the Mahabharata and sits inside the **Bhishma Parva** (the 6th parva), in chapters 23 to 40 of the BORI Critical Edition. It has 18 chapters, in which Krishna speaks to Arjuna on the battlefield of Kurukshetra.
- **The text:** this book follows the **BORI Critical Edition**, the Sanskrit Mahabharata prepared by the Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute (BORI), Pune.
- **The English translation:** by **Bibek Debroy**, from his translation of the Mahabharata (Penguin Books India). In his translation this part is called "Bhagavad Gita Parva".
- **The Hindi translation:** a Hindi translation of Debroy's English, made with the help of AI, so a mistake is possible. If you find one, please tell me.
- **Why it is cut out:** the Gita is so widely read on its own that it is given here as its own book, taken out of the Bhishma Parva. The same text is also inside the Bhishma Parva PDF in the [Mahabharata folder](../E01-Mahabharata/).
