# Mahabharata

The Mahabharata in **English and Hindi**, one PDF for each of its 18 parvas (books).

> हिन्दी: महाभारत के सभी 18 पर्वों के PDF, अंग्रेज़ी और हिन्दी दोनों में।

## About these books

- **The text:** the English version follows the **BORI Critical Edition**. This is the Sanskrit Mahabharata prepared by the Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute (BORI), Pune. Scholars worked on it for almost fifty years, from 1919 to 1966, comparing manuscripts from all over India to get the oldest text that can be recovered.
- **The English translation:** by **Bibek Debroy**, who translated the BORI Critical Edition into English (Penguin Books India, 10 volumes).
- **The Hindi translation:** a Hindi translation of Debroy's English, made for readers who find it hard to get the Mahabharata in Hindi. It was made with the help of AI, so a mistake is possible. If you find one, please tell me.
- **Layout:** each PDF is one parva. They are not the 10 Debroy volumes. This makes each book easy to find and open.

## Complete book

**Note:** `Mahabharata_Complete_English.pdf` is the **complete Mahabharata in one book**. It is not a different book from `01`, `02` and the others. Each of `01` to `18` has only **one parva**, and the complete file has **all 18 parvas together**. So if you want the whole Mahabharata, download the complete file. If you want a single parva, download its own file.

## Why this library exists

This collection is only for people who find it hard to get the Mahabharata in Hindi and English. I do not earn any money from it. If you like the books, please support the real publisher and author: buy Bibek Debroy's *The Mahabharata* from Penguin, and buy the BORI Critical Edition from the Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute.
